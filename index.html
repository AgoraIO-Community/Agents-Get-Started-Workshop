What is Agora?

Agora’s role in your voice app

Channels

A shared conversation

You and the voice agent join the same Channel to exchange audio.

Realtime network

Audio delivery

Agora’s software-defined realtime network, SDRTN, carries the live media.

Conversational AI

The agent’s response

Agora connects the Channel to the models that process speech and generate replies.

In the demo, Agora connected your microphone to the voice agent.

Use this as the bridge from the workshop promise into the technical foundation: Channels are the shared space, SDRTN delivers the media, and Agora’s cloud orchestration brings agents into that space.

Use the one-trillion-minutes proof point to establish the operating scale of Agora’s realtime network.

The quickstart uses this path. ASR is speech recognition, also called speech-to-text (STT).

Repeat a question from the opening demo and follow it across the diagram. ASR turns the question into text. The large language model (LLM) generates a reply. Text-to-speech (TTS) turns that reply into audio.

Then follow the cyan return path from right to left: the agent publishes synthesized audio back into the same Agora Channel, and the user hears it on their device.

Emphasize the boundary: SDRTN carries the realtime media path while Agora orchestrates the cascading ASR, LLM, and TTS stages as one live conversation. This is why turn detection, interruption, and latency across every stage affect how natural the agent feels.
